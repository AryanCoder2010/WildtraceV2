Wildtrace - AI-Integrated Wildlife conservation simulator

WildTrace is an AI-powered wildlife conservation platform that maps animal movement of Jaguars for example, discovers issues regarding their natural movement throughout their habitat/ Wildlife Sanctuary, etc. Runs simulations of solutions to remove those troubles, e.g. , When roads, future building projects and other future infrastructure causes problems related to their movement and their own lifestyle, our app, integrated with Gemini helps give potential solutions to those very problems, with those solutions, we can run simulations to solve the issues faced by Jaguars.
